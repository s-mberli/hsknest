# HSK Nest Launch Kit

This is a reusable documentation kit, not evidence that a release
has shipped. Use it only after the release, live-page, pricing, and account-flow
checks are complete. Do not publish from this file automatically.

## Positioning

HSK Nest is a Mandarin-first, self-hostable spaced-repetition flashcard trainer
for learners who want preloaded vocabulary review without building their own
decks. The repository supports these concrete points:

- preloaded New HSK 3.0 vocabulary for HSK 1-9, about 11,000 words;
- 3,000 example sentences with pinyin;
- a swipe-oriented Study deck, pronunciation audio where available, and FSRS
  scheduling;
- CSV/paste import for personal vocabulary;
- AGPL-3.0 open-source self-hosting; or a hosted option at €10/month or
  €99/year after a 14-day trial with no card collected at trial start.

Keep the promise narrow: convenient Mandarin vocabulary review. HSK is an
organising scaffold, not a promise of exam success, fluency, retention, or
linguistic certification. Do not claim traction, testimonials, native-speaker
validation, or superiority over other tools unless separately evidenced.

Use "Mandarin pronunciation audio" rather than "native audio". The current
audio implementation uses generated clips where configured and browser speech
as a fallback. Describe the account export as a vocabulary/progress-summary CSV,
not a complete backup or lossless export of all review history.

Destination: https://hsknest.com

Self-hosting: https://github.com/s-mberli/hsknest

## Four Weeks, 45 Minutes Per Week

The working experiment target is five learners who return within seven days of
their first session. It is a target for learning, not a forecast. A Show HN
launch replaces that week's personal outreach; do not stack channels in the
same week.

| Week | 15 minutes: reach | 15 minutes: follow-up | 15 minutes: record and decide |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Invite two relevant tutors, classmates, or Mandarin-learning contacts. | Answer replies and help only with first-session access. | Record first-session signal, obstacle, and time spent. |
| 2 | Invite two more relevant contacts, or ask one community moderator for permission. | Follow up once with Week 1 contacts who opted into it. | Record returns and choose the one recurring obstacle worth understanding. |
| 3 | If approved, make one community post; otherwise invite two more relevant contacts. | Answer replies and ask one concrete past-use question. | Record outcomes; stop or revise the message if the same obstacle repeats. |
| 4 | Post Show HN only if the product is live, tryable, and the owner can answer replies. Otherwise use two personal invites. | Stay available for replies; do not solicit votes or comments. | Count seven-day returns and write one decision for the next month. |

There is no daily posting commitment. If people try but do not return, first
investigate the recurring first-session obstacle. If reach is low, revisit the
contacts or wording before adding features or new channels.

## Posting Order and Rule Checks

1. **Existing tutor, classmate, and learner contacts.** Start with people for
   whom the Mandarin-learning context is real. Personal outreach is the lowest-
   overhead way to learn whether the first session works.
2. **One specifically chosen community, only after a moderator check.** Read
   current rules, pinned threads, self-promotion policy, link/flair/frequency
   limits, and any minimum-account or participation requirements. Ask for
   permission where required. Do not use a community that bans self-promotion.
3. **Show HN, last.** Post only when the live product is directly tryable and
   the owner can stay available for questions. Use the exact maker disclosure;
   do not present a landing page, ask friends for upvotes, or coordinate
   comments.

Before every public post:

- confirm the current URL, hosted price, trial wording, and self-host link on
  the live pages;
- check the community rules on the day, including whether a moderator reply is
  required;
- disclose ownership and any commercial option;
- use one relevant post, not repeated cross-posts or referral links;
- do not impersonate a learner, buy attention, request coordinated upvotes, or
  DM strangers;
- stop if a moderator removes the post or asks for a different format.

Current examples to recheck, not standing permission: Show HN requires a
working project people can try and says not to ask friends to upvote; the
current `r/ChineseLanguage` rules require prior moderator approval for any
self-promotion; the current `r/learnchinese` rules prohibit self-promotion or
advertising. Rules can change, so verify them again before use.
