# HSK word-list reconciliation report (Phase A)

Generated by comparing our seed data (`prisma/data/hsk/new{1..7}.json`,
10,969 words, sourced from the MIT-licensed
[`drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary`](https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary))
against the **official HSK 3.0 (2021) standard word list**, sourced from the
MIT-licensed [`ivankra/hsk30`](https://github.com/ivankra/hsk30) — a cleaned
CSV of all 11,092 official terms with level, POS, and pinyin.

## Why not the hanzistroke PDFs the user supplied?

The 7 PDFs (`HSK1-Vocabulary-3.0.pdf` … `HSK7-9-Vocabulary-3.0.pdf`) state
their own counts on the cover page — e.g. HSK1: *"300 Vocabulary Entries ·
137 Characters · 163 Words"* — which do not match the official standard's
band sizes (500/772/973/1000/1071/1140/5636 = 11,092). A per-level Hanzi
join against our seed data only matched 8–43% per band; a **level-ignoring
global** join matched 88.4%. That confirms the PDFs use their **own**
level-assignment scheme, not the official one — they're a fine cross-check
for *meanings* (used in Phase B), but not a source of truth for *which words
belong at which level*.

## Result: the word list is sound

| Metric | Count |
|---|---|
| Our words | 10,969 |
| Official words | 11,092 |
| Exact term+level matches | 10,933 (99.7% of ours) |
| Misleveled (right term, wrong band) | **0** |
| Apparent "missing" | 128 |
| Apparent "extra" | 36 |

**Zero misleveled words** — drkameleon's level assignment agrees with the
official standard on every single term we share. That was the main risk
(a word teachable at HSK 2 showing up in an HSK 5 deck); it doesn't happen.

### The 128 "missing" entries are almost all structural, not content gaps

| Category | Count | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Bound-morpheme notation, e.g. `第（第二）`, `员（服务员）` | 27 | Official lists the **morpheme** with an example compound in parens; we already have the **compound itself** as its own entry (e.g. we have 服务员 "waiter", official separately lists the bound morpheme 员). Same content, different unit of vocabulary — not a gap. |
| Grammar-pattern templates, e.g. `…极了`, `…分之…` | 2 | Official uses placeholder dots for a pattern; we have the concrete form (极了, 分之) as a word. Equivalent content. |
| Character already in our corpus under another sense/reading | 96 | Official splits polysemous characters into separate entries per reading/level (e.g. 好 as hǎo *and* hào; 种 as zhǒng *and* zhòng; 称1/称2). We have one entry per character. The character isn't missing — a second *sense* of it is unrepresented. |
| **Genuinely absent from our corpus** | **3** | See below. |

### Genuine gaps — the only actionable adds

| Term | Pinyin | Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 〇 | líng | 1 | **Not a real gap** — alternate ideographic-zero glyph for 零, which we already have at L1 with the correct "zero" gloss. Recommend: skip. |
| 表示 | biǎoshì | 2 | **Real gap.** Common verb/noun, "to express; to indicate; to show." Recommend: add to `new2.json`. |
| 森林 | sēnlín | 4 | **Real gap.** Common noun, "forest." Recommend: add to `new4.json`. |

### The 36 "extra" entries are legitimate words, not errors

Nearly all are the concrete compound-word side of the bound-morpheme split
described above (e.g. we have 科学家 "scientist", 秘书长 "secretary-general",
工艺品 "handicraft" as standalone entries; official treats 家/长/品 as the
listed unit instead). A handful are erhua-variant duplicates already in our
data alongside their non-erhua form (差点儿 + 差一点儿, both meaning "almost";
有点儿 + 有一点儿, both meaning "a bit") — harmless redundancy, not
incorrect content. **No removals recommended.**

## Recommendation

The base word list does not need reconciliation work beyond 2 additions
(表示 at L2, 森林 at L4). The two datasets model vocabulary units
differently (bound morphemes vs. compounds, single vs. multi-sense
entries per character) but agree almost completely on *content* and
*exactly* on *level placement* where they overlap. No relevel, no bulk
edits to `prisma/data/hsk/*.json` structure are warranted.

**Not yet applied** — this is a review report. Adding 表示 and 森林 requires
CC-CEDICT-sourced `{term, translation, phonetic, metadata}` entries
matching the existing seed schema; happy to draft those on request.
